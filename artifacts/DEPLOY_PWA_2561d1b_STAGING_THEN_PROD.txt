Staging functional checks:

1. Open https://staging.melato.net.in/ in a private tab and hard refresh.
2. Enable device dark mode and confirm that the application remains light.
3. Confirm category and product photos render, including placeholders where
   the source image is missing or invalid.
4. Driver: test Delivery Confirmation, Pickup Orders, and both active-dashboard
   call buttons (wholesaler and retailer).
5. Buyer: test the support call on Report Issue.
6. Wholesaler: test the driver call on Pickup Orders.
7. Test the call action in Support Center.
8. Confirm the call confirmation dialog appears and the phone dialer opens on
   a real mobile device.

After all staging checks pass, run the cancel script printed by the deployment
script. Verify that no matching rollback timer remains:

systemctl list-timers --all | grep 'ekd-staging-pwa-princy-ui-rollback' || \
  echo 'No staging PWA rollback timer remains'
