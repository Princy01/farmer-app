Melato - Quick User Guide
Essential How-to-Use Guide for Wholesalers, Retailers, and Drivers
English Edition
2-Page Quick Summary
1. Getting Started & Account Setup All Users
🔑 Login & Sign Up

Select role (Wholesaler, Retailer, Driver), enter Mobile Number/Email, and verify with OTP.

📋 Business & KYC Info

Enter Business Name, Owner Name, GST, PAN, Aadhaar, and Government License Number.

📍 Locations & Language

Add branch/shop delivery addresses under Business Locations. Switch English/Hindi in Settings.

2. Retailer (Buyer) Guide - How to Order & Manage Purchases Retailer
Step 1: Browse & Search
Open Home screen, search produce by name, browse categories, and check prices/stock.
Step 2: Cart & Units
Add items to Cart, choose units (KG, Quintal, Ton, gm), and select your delivery location.
Step 3: Checkout & Pay
Review order at Checkout, choose payment method (Online / UPI / Cards / COD), and get Order ID.
Step 4: Orders & Disputes
Track order status in Order History. Report any damaged or short items under Report Issue.
3. Wholesaler Guide - Stock Management & Order Fulfillment Wholesaler
🏠 Home Dashboard & Stock Logic
  • Live Metrics: View Today's Earnings, This Week's Revenue, Pending Payouts, Total Stock & Active Orders.
  • Automatic Stock Deduction: When a buyer places an order, quantity automatically deducts from your available stock in real time.
  • Recently in Demand: View trending commodities requested by retailers.
📦 Stock Dashboard & Add/Update
  • Stock Dashboard: View all products in your inventory with live stock levels (In Stock, Needs Attention, Out of Stock).
  • Add Stock: Tap Add Stock to enter commodity name, category, unit (KG, gm, Quintal, Ton), price, and available quantity.
  • Update Stock: Quickly adjust available quantities or prices as inventory changes.
📑 Orders Received & Pickups
  • Orders Received: View incoming retailer orders, review items, quantities, buyer details, and accept/prepare for dispatch.
  • Pickup Orders: View packed orders ready for collection and hand them over to the driver upon arrival.
📈 Demand Insights & Earnings
  • Next-Day Demand & Restocking: View predicted demand for tomorrow and recommendations on what to restock.
  • Trends & Earnings: View sales/demand trends, market price comparisons, and track completed/pending payouts.

4. Driver Guide - Deliveries & Earnings Driver
Step 1: Registration
Enter driver details under Driver Registration, upload DL/ID proof, and enter vehicle details.
Step 2: Accept Requests
Open Transport Requests to view jobs with pickup/drop locations & fare. Tap Accept.
Step 3: Pickup Orders
Go to wholesaler location under Pickup Orders, check package count, and confirm pickup in the app.
Step 4: Delivery & Earnings
Deliver goods to retailer and confirm delivery under Delivery Confirmation. View earnings in Earnings Dashboard.
5. In-App Help Center & Support Center Settings > Help / Support
📖 Interactive Help Center
  • Interactive Walkthrough: Quick tutorial explaining core tasks. Tap Replay Walkthrough to view anytime.
  • Category Filters: Filter guides by role tasks (Shopping, Orders, Deliveries, Stock, Earnings).
  • Direct Screen Jump: Tap any help card (e.g. "Browse and search", "Update stock", "Confirm delivery") to open that exact screen immediately.
📞 Support Center
  • Select Issue Category: Choose from Payment, Order, Incoming Order, Delivery, Dispatch, Trip, Goods, Seller, Account, Technical, Safety.
  • Select Problem & Contact: Pick the specific issue from the list, add details, and contact support directly for quick resolution.
  • Order Disputes: Raise item/delivery disputes directly under Report Issue on any order.
