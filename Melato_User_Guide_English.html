Melato - How to Use Guide
A simple, step-by-step guide explaining the features available in Melato for Wholesalers, Retailers, and Drivers, and how to access and use them.

🛒 Retailer (Buyer)

Browse fresh produce, place bulk orders, track order history & manage spends.

🏢 Wholesaler

Manage stock, process incoming retailer orders, view demand & track earnings.

🚚 Driver

Accept delivery requests, manage pickups, confirm deliveries & check trip earnings.

1. Getting Started & Account Setup (All Users)

Setup
🔑 Login & Sign Up
  • Open the app and select your role: Wholesaler, Retailer, or Driver.
  • Enter your registered Mobile Number or Email Address.
  • Enter the OTP received on your phone/email to log in securely.
  • New users can switch to the "Register" tab to create an account.
📋 Business Registration & KYC Info
  • Business Details: Enter Business Name, Owner Name, Business Category, Business Type, and Established Year.
  • Legal & Tax Details: Enter Mobile Number, Email, GST Number, PAN Number, Aadhaar Number, and Government License Number.
  • Update Business Info: Update contact info, business status (Active/Inactive), and address anytime under Business Info.
📍 Business Locations
  • View all registered shop or warehouse locations.
  • Tap Add New Location to add branch addresses (City, State, Full Address, Contact, Email, GST, PAN).
  • Retailers can select which location orders should be delivered to, and Wholesalers can manage where stock is stored.
⚙️ Settings & Language Selection
  • Language: Switch between English and Hindi (हिन्दी) anytime.
  • Profile & Security: Manage profile information and log out securely.

2. Retailer (Buyer) Guide

Retailer
🔍 Home Dashboard & Product Search
  • Browse Categories: View products grouped by categories (Vegetables, Fruits, etc.).
  • Search Items: Use the search bar to find items quickly by name.
  • Product Details: View unit pricing, available stock quantity, and minimum order requirements.
🛒 Cart & Units Management
  • Add items to cart and adjust required quantities.
  • Supports standard agri units (KG, Quintal, Ton, gm).
  • Review subtotal, taxes, delivery charges, and total order cost.
  • Select the delivery Business Location where goods should be sent.
💳 Checkout & Payment
  • Review your order summary and delivery address.
  • Choose your preferred payment method (Online Payment / UPI / Cards / Cash or Credit terms as available).
  • Tap Place Order to receive an instant order confirmation screen with Order ID.
📦 Retailer Order History & Order Details
  • Track Active Orders: View the current status of placed orders (Pending, Confirmed, Ready, In Transit, Delivered, Cancelled).
  • Order Details: Open any order to view full line-item details, pricing breakdown, supplier info, and order date/time.
  • Past Orders: Access complete history of previous purchases.
📊 Spends & Retailer Trends
  • Spends: View total expenditure summary over time to track your procurement budget.
  • Retailer Trends: Check price and purchase trends to make smarter buying decisions.
⚠️ Report an Issue / Disputes
  • If there is an issue with your order (damaged goods, wrong item, quantity shortage, or delivery issue), tap Report Issue on the order.
  • Select the issue category, describe the problem, and submit the ticket.
  • Track the status and resolution of your ticket in My Issues.

3. Wholesaler Guide

Wholesaler
🏠 Wholesaler Home Dashboard
  • Quick Stats: View Today's Earnings, This Week's Revenue, Pending Payouts, Total Stock, and Active Orders.
  • Stock Deduction on Order: When a buyer places an order, the stock automatically gets deducted from your total available stock in real time.
  • Recently in Demand: View trending products frequently ordered by retailers.
📦 Stock Dashboard & Add / Update Stock
  • Stock Dashboard: View all products in your inventory with current quantities (In Stock, Needs Attention, Out of Stock).
  • Add Stock: Add new commodities, set product name, category, unit (KG, gm, Quintal, Ton), price per unit, and available quantity.
  • Update Stock: Quickly adjust available quantities or prices as stock changes.
📑 Orders Received & Order Details
  • Orders Received: View list of orders placed by retailers, review ordered items, quantities, buyer details, and accept/prepare orders for dispatch.
🚚 Pickup Orders
  • View orders that are packed and ready for driver collection.
  • Coordinate hand-off with the driver when they arrive for pickup.
📈 Demand Insights, Trends & Earnings
  • Next-Day Demand & Past Demand: View predicted demand for tomorrow to prepare stock in advance.
  • Restocking Recommendations: Check recommendations on which items need restocking.
  • Trends & Market Opportunities: View sales trends, demand trends, stock insights, and market price comparisons.
  • Earnings Dashboard: Monitor gross earnings, completed payouts, and pending balances.
⚠️ Wholesaler Disputes & Issues
  • Report any order or pickup problem directly from the order screen.
  • Track investigation status and resolution history under My Issues.

4. Driver Guide

Driver
📝 Driver & Vehicle Registration
  • Enter Driver Basic Information (Name, Mobile Number, Experience).
  • Upload required documents (Driving License, ID Proof).
  • Enter Vehicle Details (Vehicle Type, Registration Number, Capacity).
📊 Driver Dashboard & Transport Requests
  • Dashboard: View active delivery tasks, completed trips, and today's summary.
  • Transport Requests: View incoming delivery job requests with pickup location, drop location, load details, and estimated fare.
  • Accept Requests: Drivers can directly accept available delivery requests to take up the job.
📦 Pickup Orders
  • View the list of accepted orders waiting for pickup at wholesaler locations.
  • Navigate to the wholesaler address, verify package count, and confirm pickup in the app.
✅ Delivery Confirmation
  • Navigate to the retailer's delivery location with the shipment.
  • Confirm order hand-off and complete delivery in the app.
  • The delivery status is updated to completed immediately for all parties.
💰 Earnings Dashboard & Delivery History
  • Earnings Dashboard: Track earnings for all completed trips and view payout summaries.
  • Delivery History: View log of all past completed deliveries with date, route, and fare.
  • Disputes: Report any trip or pickup problems under Report Issue and track them in My Issues.

5. In-App Help Center & Support Center

Help & Support
📖 Interactive Help Center

The Help Center is built into the app for every role (Wholesaler, Retailer, Driver) under Settings to provide instant step-by-step guidance:

  • Visual Walkthrough: Shows an introductory step-by-step tutorial explaining core app tasks. Tap Replay Walkthrough to view it again anytime.
  • Category Filters: Filter guides by category:
    • Retailer: Shopping, Orders, Support, Account
    • Wholesaler: Stock, Orders, Insights, Support
    • Driver: Getting Started, Deliveries, Earnings, Support
  • Direct Screen Navigation: Tapping any Help Card (e.g. "Browse and search", "Manage your cart", "Update stock", "Review transport requests", "Confirm delivery") directly opens that screen in the app so you can perform the task immediately.
📞 Support Center

When you need assistance from the support team, open the Support Center under Settings:

  • Choose Issue Category: Select from categories such as Payment Issues, Order Issues, Incoming Order Issues, Delivery Issues, Dispatch & Pickup, Trip & Assignment, Goods / Product Issues, Seller Issues, Account Issues, Technical Issues, or Safety.
  • Select Specific Problem & Contact Directly: Choose the specific problem description from the list, add any optional details, and contact the support team directly for assistance and quick resolution.
